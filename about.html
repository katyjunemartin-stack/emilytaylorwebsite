Why Real Estate?

“I think Real Estate chose me.”

More than a career

Over the years, people have asked Emily what made her choose real estate. Her answer has changed a little over time, but the heart of it has always stayed the same.

She wanted to be a trusted Realtor — someone people could fully depend on during one of the most important milestones of their lives. She wanted to exceed expectations through hard work, tenacity and grit, while helping change the way the public perceives real estate professionals.

And somewhere along the way, she realized that she didn't simply want a career. She wanted a lifestyle that allowed her to be needed, challenged and involved.

“Truthfully, I thrive when I'm needed — when the phone rings at 10 p.m., when an offer is accepted at 11:59 p.m.”

That's what makes real estate feel different for Emily. It's personal. It's unpredictable. It's demanding. And when everything comes together for a client, it's incredibly rewarding.

What started as a career became something much bigger. A mindset. A purpose. A way of showing up for people.

How It Started

Built from,
the ground up.

Emily got her start in this business by door knocking in -20°C weather, cold calling For Sale By Owners and homeowners around active listings.

She listened to successful agents and jumped at every opportunity to learn and grow. Every day was a new opportunity to meet someone, help someone and become better at what she did.

Rejection was part of the process. Instead of letting it stop her, Emily pushed through it and developed a thick skin early on — something she jokingly refers to as her “lizard skin.”

Her business began to grow, and so did her referral base. Referrals and repeat clients became the heart of the business and continue to represent a large portion of her business each year.

Real estate also gave Emily an unshakable mindset, one that continues to bring strength and gratitude to her life every day.

Hard work builds more than a business.

It builds resilience, confidence, perspective and the ability to keep showing up when it matters.
