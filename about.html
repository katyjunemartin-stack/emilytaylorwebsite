She thrives in the moments when clients need someone dependable — someone who will answer the phone, explain what comes next and remain calm when the process becomes complicated.

HOW IT STARTED

Built from the ground up.

Emily's early days in real estate were not glamorous. They meant door knocking in -20°C weather, cold calling FSBOs and homeowners, learning the business from the ground up and becoming comfortable with rejection.

Every conversation, every closed door and every difficult transaction became part of the foundation. She learned quickly that success in real estate is built through consistency, resilience and genuinely caring about the people on the other side of the transaction.

Over time, those relationships became referrals and repeat clients. The business grew through trust — one client and one conversation at a time.

Hard work builds more than a business.
