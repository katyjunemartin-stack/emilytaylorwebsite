How It Started

Start with grit.
Keep going.

Emily got her start in this business from door knocking in -20 weather and cold calling “for sale by owners” and homeowners around listings.

She listened to successful agents and jumped at every chance to learn and grow. She viewed each day as a new opportunity to help someone.

She pushed through rejection and formed a thick skin early on — often referred to as her “lizard skin.”

Her business began to grow, as did her referral base. Referrals and repeat clients truly are the heart of her business and represent a large portion of her business each year.

Real estate caused Emily to adopt an unshakable mindset, one that brings strength and gratitude to her life daily.
