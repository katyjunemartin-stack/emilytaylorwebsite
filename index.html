Born and raised in Peterborough, with years spent in Toronto, Emily brings a perspective shaped by both local roots and broader market experience.

Her approach to real estate is built around relationships, communication and being there when her clients need her most.

From first-time buyers to families making a major move, seniors downsizing, executors managing an estate or homeowners preparing to sell, Emily believes every client deserves thoughtful, personal representation.
