Born and raised in Peterborough, with many years spent in Toronto, Emily brings a diverse professional background and a genuine passion for people to her real estate career.

Her business was built through hard work, persistence and a willingness to do whatever it took to learn, grow and serve her clients well.

Today, relationships and referrals remain at the heart of her business. Emily believes the best representation isn't simply about getting a transaction completed. It's about being someone her clients can genuinely depend on.
