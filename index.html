Meet Emily

I think real estate chose me.

Born and raised in Peterborough and having spent many years in Toronto, Emily brings a broad understanding of the communities she serves and the different reasons people choose to call them home.

Since 2017, Emily has helped more than 100 families work toward their real estate goals. Her business has been built through relationships, referrals and repeat clients — people who trusted her enough to come back and trusted her enough to recommend her to someone they care about.

Her approach is rooted in hard work, tenacity and grit. Emily believes clients deserve a REALTOR® who is available, prepared and genuinely invested in the outcome.
