Born and raised in Peterborough, with many years spent in Toronto, Emily brings a diverse professional background and a genuine passion for real estate, people, travel and the communities she serves.

Her business is built around relationships, results and being there when her clients need her most. From Toronto and Durham Region to Vaughan, Kawartha Lakes and Peterborough, Emily works to make every client feel prioritized, supported, respected and valued.
