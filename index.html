Meet Emily

“I think real estate chose me.”

Emily got her start in real estate by door knocking in -20° weather and cold calling for-sale-by-owners and homeowners around listings.

She listened to successful agents, jumped at every opportunity to learn and grow, and learned early that rejection was simply part of the process.

That persistence helped shape what she jokingly refers to as her “lizard skin” — an unshakable mindset that continues to bring strength and gratitude to her life and business.

Over the years, her business grew alongside her referral base. Referrals and repeat clients remain at the heart of her business and represent a large portion of her work each year.

Her goal has always remained the same: to be the kind of Realtor people can truly depend on during one of the most important milestones of their lives.
