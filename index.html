04 — ABOUT EMILY

I think real estate chose me.

Born and raised in Peterborough and having spent many years in Toronto, Emily brings a diverse professional background and a genuine passion for people to her real estate career.

Since 2017, she has helped more than 100 families achieve their real estate goals throughout Toronto, Durham Region, Vaughan, Kawartha Lakes and Peterborough.

Her business is built around relationships, referrals and repeat clients — people who know they can call when they need someone in their corner.

“I want to be a trusted realtor, one who people can fully depend on. I want to be there every step of the way, during one of the most important milestones of their lives.”

Emily specializes in seniors, families, executors, powers of attorney and first-time buyers, while also providing full service listing preparation, staging and negotiation.
